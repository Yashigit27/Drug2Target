How D2T works

Evidence-first target discovery in three stages

Each stage produces inspectable artifacts so you can trace every ranked target back to data sources.

01

Resolve disease

Map disease, subtype, and tissue to MONDO/EFO ontology identifiers with Open Targets search.

02

Collect evidence

Merge Open Targets associations with literature RAG gene extraction from Europe PMC abstracts.

03

Dual-track rank

Track A scores underexplored known targets. Track B expands STRING neighborhoods for novel candidates.
